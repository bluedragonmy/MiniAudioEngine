Digital sound數位聲音

Your DAW never stores a wave — only snapshots of it. Take too few snapshots and high notes don't disappear: they come back as the wrong notes. DAW 從來不會儲存一整條波，只會存它的「快照」。快照拍得太少，高音不會消失，而是變成錯誤的音跑回來。

Snapshots of a wave波的快照

A computer can't store a smooth curve. Instead it measures the wave thousands of times per second and stores each measurement as a number — a sample. How many samples per second is the sample rate. CD audio uses 44,100 samples per second (44.1 kHz); video and most DAW projects use 48 kHz. 電腦沒辦法儲存一條平滑的曲線。它的做法是每秒量測波形好幾千次，把每次量到的值存成一個數字，這叫做一個取樣（sample）。每秒取樣幾次，就是取樣率。CD 用的是每秒 44,100 次（44.1 kHz），影片和大部分 DAW 專案用 48 kHz。

The Nyquist limitNyquist 上限

To capture a wave you need at least two samples per cycle — one for the peak and one for the trough. So a sample rate can only hold frequencies up to half of itself. That ceiling is called the Nyquist frequency. At 44.1 kHz it's 22.05 kHz, just above the top of human hearing — which is why audio sample rates start around 44 kHz. 要記錄一個波，每個週期至少需要兩個取樣點：一個在波峰，一個在波谷。所以一個取樣率最多只能記錄到它自己一半的頻率，這個上限叫做 Nyquist 頻率。44.1 kHz 的 Nyquist 是 22.05 kHz，剛好高於人耳的上限，這也是音訊取樣率大多從 44 kHz 左右起跳的原因。

Aliasing: notes that fold backAliasing：折回來的音

What happens to a frequency above Nyquist? It doesn't vanish. The samples line up with a lower wave instead — the dashed line above passes through exactly the same dots. Your ear can't tell the difference, so you hear the low one. This impostor is called an alias. 超過 Nyquist 的頻率會怎樣？它不會消失。那些取樣點會剛好落在一條比較低的波上，上面那條虛線就穿過完全相同的點。耳朵分不出差別，所以你聽到的是那個低的音。這個冒牌貨就叫做 alias（混疊）。

The alias isn't random. Frequencies fold back at Nyquist like a mirror: at 8 kHz sample rate, 5 kHz comes back as 3 kHz, 7 kHz as 1 kHz. Sweep a tone upward and, past Nyquist, its pitch goes down. alias 不是隨機的。頻率在 Nyquist 像照鏡子一樣折回來：取樣率 8 kHz 時，5 kHz 會變成 3 kHz，7 kHz 會變成 1 kHz。把一個音往上掃，超過 Nyquist 之後，音高反而會往下掉。

Where you'll meet aliasing in a real project在真實專案裡，你會在哪裡遇到 aliasing

Your audio interface filters out everything above Nyquist before sampling, so recordings are safe. The trouble starts inside the DAW: distortion, saturation and clipping create new high harmonics, and the ones above Nyquist fold back as harsh, out-of-tune noise. That's what the oversampling switch on many plugins is for — it runs the effect at a higher sample rate, filters, and comes back down. 錄音介面在取樣前會先把 Nyquist 以上的頻率濾掉，所以錄進來的聲音是安全的。問題出在 DAW 裡面：失真、飽和、削波會產生新的高頻泛音，超過 Nyquist 的部分會折回來，變成刺耳、不在調上的雜音。許多外掛上的 oversampling（超取樣）開關就是為此而設計：先在更高的取樣率處理效果，濾波之後再降回來。

Now try it in your DAW換你在 DAW 裡試試
  1. Find your project's sample rate (Ableton: Preferences → Audio, FL Studio: Options → Audio settings, Logic: Project Settings → Audio). It's probably 44.1 or 48 kHz. 找到你專案的取樣率（Ableton：Preferences → Audio，FL Studio：Options → Audio settings，Logic：Project Settings → Audio），大概是 44.1 或 48 kHz。
  2. Put a downsampling effect on a synth or drum loop (Ableton: Redux, Logic: Bitcrusher, or any free bitcrusher plugin with a sample-rate control) and lower the rate slowly. The metallic, out-of-tune tones you hear are aliases. 在合成器或鼓 loop 上加一個降取樣效果器（Ableton：Redux，Logic：Bitcrusher，或任何有取樣率旋鈕的免費 bitcrusher 外掛），慢慢把取樣率調低。你聽到那些金屬感、走音的聲音，就是 alias。
  3. Next time you load a distortion or saturator, look for an oversampling option. Push the drive hard and compare it on and off. 下次載入失真或飽和外掛時，找找看有沒有 oversampling 選項。把 drive 推大，比較開和關的差別。

Key takeaways重點整理

  • Digital audio is a list of samples; the sample rate is how many per second.數位音訊是一串取樣；取樣率是每秒取樣幾次。
  • A sample rate can only hold frequencies up to half of itself: the Nyquist frequency.取樣率最多只能記錄它一半以下的頻率，這叫做 Nyquist 頻率。
  • Anything above Nyquist folds back as an alias. In a mix, that's usually distortion without oversampling.超過 Nyquist 的頻率會折回來，變成 alias。在混音裡，最常見的來源是沒開 oversampling 的失真效果。
