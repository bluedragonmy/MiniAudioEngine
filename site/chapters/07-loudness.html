Loudness響度

Play two identical mixes, one slightly louder, and almost everyone prefers the louder one. That one fact explains a whole era of over-squashed music, and why it doesn't work any more. 播放兩個一模一樣的混音，其中一個稍微大聲一點，幾乎每個人都會選大聲的那個。光是這件事，就解釋了一整個時代被壓得扁扁的音樂，以及為什麼這招現在已經沒用了。

Louder sounds better (even when it isn't)大聲聽起來比較好（即使其實沒有）

Listen to A and B below and pick the one you like better. Switch back and forth as much as you want. 聽聽下面的 A 和 B，選出你比較喜歡的那個。想切換幾次都可以。

I prefer:我比較喜歡：

Our ears get more sensitive to bass and treble as the volume rises, so a louder copy seems fuller and brighter — even though nothing about the mix changed. This is why you should always compare things (two mixes, a plugin on and off, your track and a reference) at matched loudness. 音量越大，耳朵對低音和高音越敏感，所以大聲的版本聽起來比較飽滿、比較亮，雖然混音本身完全沒有變。這就是為什麼比較任何東西（兩個混音版本、外掛開和關、你的歌和參考曲）時，一定要在響度對齊的狀態下比。

Peak is not loudness峰值不等於響度

A peak meter shows the single highest sample. But our ears care about how much energy there is over time. Below, all three sounds peak at exactly −1 dBFS. Play them. 峰值表顯示的是最高的那一個取樣點，但耳朵在意的是一段時間內有多少能量。下面三個聲音的峰值都剛好是 −1 dBFS，播放聽聽看。

That's what LUFS (Loudness Units relative to Full Scale) measures. It's defined by the broadcast standard ITU-R BS.1770: filter the audio to roughly match how our ears weigh frequencies, take the average energy, and ignore near-silent parts. Like dBFS, the numbers are negative and closer to 0 means louder. 這就是 LUFS（相對於滿刻度的響度單位）在量的東西。它由廣播標準 ITU-R BS.1770 定義：先用濾波器模擬耳朵對各頻率的敏感度，再取平均能量，並忽略幾乎無聲的部分。跟 dBFS 一樣，數字是負的，越接近 0 越大聲。

Same peak, different loudness同樣的峰值，不同的響度

The bars show integrated loudness in LUFS. The peak column is identical for all three. 長條顯示的是整體響度（LUFS）。三者的峰值欄位完全一樣。

What streaming does to your master串流平台會對你的母帶做什麼

Streaming services measure every song's LUFS and turn it to a common target so listeners don't have to keep reaching for the volume. Spotify aims for about −14 LUFS by default, YouTube about −14, Apple Music about −16. If your master is louder, it simply gets turned down. 串流平台會量每首歌的 LUFS，把它調到一個共同的目標，聽眾就不用一直調音量。Spotify 預設大約是 −14 LUFS，YouTube 約 −14，Apple Music 約 −16。如果你的母帶比較大聲，它就只會被調小。

So what happens if you crank a master to be as loud as possible? Drag the slider: more drive means more of the waveform gets clipped. Then switch to "after Spotify" to hear what a listener actually gets. 那如果把母帶推到能有多大聲就多大聲，會怎樣？拉動滑桿：drive 越多，波形被削掉的部分就越多。再切換到「Spotify 處理後」，聽聽聽眾實際聽到的是什麼。

Real mastering uses limiters, which are much cleaner than this hard clipper, but the trade is the same: every extra dB of loudness costs dynamics, and on streaming services you don't keep the loudness anyway. 真正的母帶處理會用 limiter，比這裡的硬削波乾淨很多，但代價是一樣的：多換一分響度，就少一分動態，而且在串流平台上，多出來的響度反正也留不住。

Now try it in your DAW換你在 DAW 裡試試
  1. Put a loudness meter on your master. Logic has one built in (Metering → Loudness Meter); for any DAW, the free Youlean Loudness Meter works well. 在 master 上加一個響度表。Logic 有內建的（Metering → Loudness Meter）；任何 DAW 都可以用免費的 Youlean Loudness Meter。
  2. Play a commercial track you like and read its integrated LUFS, then play yours. That gap is what you're actually comparing when "theirs sounds better". 播放一首你喜歡的市售歌曲，看它的整體 LUFS，再播你自己的歌。當你覺得「人家的比較好聽」時，你比較的其實就是這個差距。
  3. Before judging any plugin, turn its output down until switching it on and off doesn't change the loudness. Does it still sound better? 評估任何外掛之前，先把它的輸出調小，直到開關它時響度不變。這樣還是比較好聽嗎？

Key takeaways重點整理

  • Louder sounds better to our ears, so always compare at matched loudness.耳朵會覺得大聲比較好聽，所以比較時一定要響度對齊。
  • Peak = highest sample. LUFS = perceived loudness over time.峰值＝最高的取樣點。LUFS＝一段時間內感受到的響度。
  • Streaming turns loud masters down to around −14 LUFS; extra loudness only costs you dynamics.串流平台會把大聲的母帶調小到約 −14 LUFS，多出來的響度只會讓你失去動態。

Under the hood: cpp/src/LoudnessMeter.cpp implements BS.1770 K-weighting and EBU R128 gating; its tests check the standard's reference value (a 997 Hz sine at 0 dBFS reads −3.01 LUFS). 技術細節：cpp/src/LoudnessMeter.cpp 實作了 BS.1770 的 K-weighting 和 EBU R128 的 gating；測試會檢查標準規定的參考值（997 Hz、0 dBFS 正弦波＝−3.01 LUFS）。
