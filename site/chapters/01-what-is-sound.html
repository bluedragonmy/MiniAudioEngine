Chapter 1第 1 章

What is sound?聲音是什麼？

Every knob in your DAW changes one of two things about a wave: how fast it moves, or how big it is. Let's hear both. DAW 裡的每一顆旋鈕，改變的都是波的兩件事之一：它振動得多快，或是振動得多大。我們來親耳聽聽看。

Sound is air pressure moving back and forth. A speaker cone pushes the air, pulls it back, pushes again — and your eardrum follows along. If we draw that pressure over time, we get a waveform. 聲音就是空氣壓力的來回變化。喇叭振膜把空氣往前推、再拉回來、再推出去，你的耳膜就跟著一起振動。把這個壓力隨時間的變化畫出來，就是波形。

The simplest waveform is the sine wave: a pure tone with nothing else in it. Press play and move the sliders. 最簡單的波形是正弦波：一個純粹、沒有任何雜質的音。按下播放，然後拉拉看滑桿。

Frequency is pitch頻率就是音高

Frequency is how many times the wave repeats per second, measured in hertz (Hz). More cycles per second sounds higher. Watch the canvas: at 100 Hz you see one cycle in 10 ms; at 1000 Hz you see ten. 頻率是波每秒重複的次數，單位是赫茲（Hz）。每秒重複越多次，聽起來就越高。看看上面的圖：100 Hz 時，10 毫秒裡只有一個週期；1000 Hz 時就有十個。

Here's the surprise: our ears hear pitch in ratios, not steps. Doubling the frequency always sounds like the same jump — one octave. Play these four: they're all the note A. 有趣的地方來了：耳朵聽音高是看倍數，不是看相差幾 Hz。頻率變成兩倍，聽起來永遠是同樣的距離，也就是一個八度。播放下面四個音，它們全都是 A。

Octaves of AA 的各個八度

110 → 220 is a gap of 110 Hz; 440 → 880 is a gap of 440 Hz. Same musical distance. That's why the frequency slider above (and every EQ in your DAW) uses a log scale. 110 → 220 差了 110 Hz，440 → 880 差了 440 Hz，但在音樂上是同樣的距離。這就是為什麼上面的頻率滑桿（以及 DAW 裡所有的 EQ）都用對數刻度。

Humans hear roughly 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz (the top end drops with age). In mixing you'll hear people talk about ranges: lows (kick, bass, below ~250 Hz), mids (vocals, guitars, ~250 Hz–4 kHz) and highs (cymbals, air, above ~4 kHz). 人耳大約聽得到 20 Hz 到 20,000 Hz（高頻上限會隨年齡下降）。混音時大家常講幾個頻段：低頻（kick、貝斯，約 250 Hz 以下）、中頻（人聲、吉他，約 250 Hz–4 kHz）、高頻（鈸、空氣感，約 4 kHz 以上）。

Amplitude is loudness振幅就是音量

Amplitude is how far the wave swings from the middle line. Bigger swings push more air and sound louder. Drag the amplitude slider and watch the wave grow and shrink — the pitch doesn't change. 振幅是波離開中線擺動的幅度。擺得越大，推動的空氣越多，聽起來就越大聲。拖動振幅滑桿，看波形變大變小，但音高不會改變。

Like pitch, loudness works in ratios, so audio uses decibels (dB). Halving the amplitude is always about −6 dB, whether you start loud or quiet. Try it: 100% → 50% → 25% shows 0, −6 and −12 dB. 跟音高一樣，音量也是看倍數，所以音訊用分貝（dB）來表示。振幅減半永遠大約是 −6 dB，不管原本多大聲。試試看：100% → 50% → 25%，會顯示 0、−6、−12 dB。

Why 0 dB is the top為什麼 0 dB 是最大值

In a DAW, meters count down from 0 dBFS ("full scale") — the biggest value a digital file can hold. Push past it and the top of the wave gets chopped off: clipping, which sounds harsh and crunchy. That's why a healthy mix lives in negative numbers. We'll come back to this in the mixing chapters. 在 DAW 裡，音量表是從 0 dBFS（Full Scale，滿刻度）往下算，那是數位檔案能存的最大值。超過它，波形頂端就會被削掉，這叫做削波（clipping），聽起來會刺耳、有破音。所以健康的混音音量都在負數。之後的混音章節會再回來談這件事。

Train your ears練練耳朵

You'll hear two short tones. Which one is higher? The gap is between 1 and 4 semitones, so some rounds are tricky. 你會聽到兩個短音，哪一個比較高？兩個音相差 1 到 4 個半音，所以有幾題會有點難。

Now try it in your DAW換你在 DAW 裡試試
  1. Load a basic synth (Ableton: Operator, FL Studio: 3x Osc, Logic: Retro Synth) and pick a sine wave. 載入一個基本合成器（Ableton：Operator，FL Studio：3x Osc，Logic：Retro Synth），選正弦波。
  2. Put a spectrum analyzer after it (Ableton: Spectrum, FL Studio: Fruity Parametric EQ 2, Logic: Channel EQ with the analyzer on). 在後面加一個頻譜分析器（Ableton：Spectrum，FL Studio：Fruity Parametric EQ 2，Logic：開啟分析器的 Channel EQ）。
  3. Play A4. You'll see a single spike at 440 Hz. Play A3 — the spike moves to 220 Hz. Turn the volume down 6 dB and watch the meter. 彈 A4，你會看到 440 Hz 有一根尖峰。彈 A3，尖峰會移到 220 Hz。把音量調低 6 dB，看看音量表的變化。

Key takeaways重點整理

  • Frequency (Hz) = pitch. Double it and you go up one octave.頻率（Hz）＝音高。變成兩倍就是高一個八度。
  • Amplitude = loudness, measured in dB. Half the amplitude ≈ −6 dB.振幅＝音量，用 dB 表示。振幅減半約等於 −6 dB。
  • Digital audio tops out at 0 dBFS. Above that: clipping.數位音訊的上限是 0 dBFS，超過就會削波。
