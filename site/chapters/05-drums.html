Drums & rhythm鼓與節奏

Classic drum machines like the 808 never recorded a real drum. They built every hit from the pieces you already know: oscillators, noise, filters and envelopes. 像 808 這樣的經典鼓機，從來沒有錄過真正的鼓。每一聲打擊都是用你已經學過的零件組出來的：振盪器、噪音、濾波器和包絡。

Three recipes三份食譜

One new ingredient first: noise. It's random numbers, so it contains every frequency at once — the sound of static or a "shhh". Pitched sounds come from oscillators; hissy sounds come from noise. 先介紹一個新材料：噪音。噪音就是一串亂數，同時包含了所有頻率，聽起來像收音機的雜訊或「噓——」的聲音。有音高的聲音來自振盪器，嘶嘶聲則來自噪音。

  • Kick = a sine wave whose pitch drops fast. The drop is the "punch"; the low tail is the "boom".Kick（大鼓）＝音高快速下降的正弦波。下降的瞬間是「衝擊感」，低沉的尾巴是「轟」的那一聲。
  • Snare = a short tone (the drum skin) + high-passed noise (the rattling wires underneath).Snare（小鼓）＝短促的音（鼓皮）＋高通過的噪音（鼓底下那排響弦的沙沙聲）。
  • Hi-hat = noise through a high-pass filter, very short. Make it longer and it becomes an open hat.Hi-hat（腳踏鈸）＝經過高通濾波的噪音，非常短。拉長一點就變成開放的 hi-hat。

Try the kick's pitch drop: very fast gives a tight, clicky electronic kick; slower turns it into a laser "pew". And on the snare, slide snappy all the way down — what's left sounds like a tom. 試試 kick 的音高下降：非常快會得到緊實、有點「喀」聲的電子大鼓；慢一點就變成雷射槍的「咻」。把 snare 的響弦量拉到最低，剩下的聲音就像通鼓（tom）。

Putting hits on a grid把打擊放到格子上

Most music counts in bars of four beats. Split each beat into four and you get 16 steps per bar — the grid every drum machine and DAW step sequencer uses. BPM (beats per minute) sets how fast you move through it: at 120 BPM a beat lasts 0.5 s and a step lasts 0.125 s. 大部分音樂以「一小節四拍」來數。把每一拍再切成四份，一小節就有 16 個步（step），所有鼓機和 DAW 的步進音序器都用這個格子。BPM（每分鐘幾拍）決定走過格子的速度：120 BPM 時，一拍 0.5 秒，一步 0.125 秒。

Program a beat編一段節奏

Click squares to add or remove hits while it plays. The kit uses the sounds you built above. A closed hat cuts off a ringing open hat, just like a real hi-hat pedal (this is called a choke). 播放時點格子就能加入或拿掉打擊，用的是你上面組好的鼓聲。閉合 hi-hat 會切掉正在響的開放 hi-hat，就像真的 hi-hat 踏板一樣（這叫做 choke）。

The three presets show the most common skeleton in pop music: kick on the strong beats, snare on beats 2 and 4 (the "backbeat"), and hats filling in between. Change only where the kicks land and the genre changes with it. 三個預設展示了流行音樂最常見的骨架：kick 在重拍、snare 在第 2 和第 4 拍（所謂的「反拍」backbeat）、hi-hat 填在中間。光是改變 kick 落在哪裡，曲風就跟著變了。

Swing: the human feelSwing：人味

A drummer never plays perfectly on the grid. Swing delays every second 16th note a little, turning straight "1-e-and-a" into a bouncing "lo-ng short lo-ng short". Turn it up on the boom bap pattern and hear it start to groove. 鼓手從來不會打得跟格子一模一樣。Swing 會把每兩個 16 分音符中的第二個稍微往後延，讓平直的「噠噠噠噠」變成有彈性的「噠——噠、噠——噠」。在 boom bap 節奏上把它調高，聽聽律動感出來的感覺。

Now try it in your DAW換你在 DAW 裡試試
  1. Open the step sequencer: FL Studio's Channel Rack, Ableton's Drum Rack with a MIDI clip, or Logic's Step Sequencer. Copy one of the patterns above. 打開步進音序器：FL Studio 的 Channel Rack、Ableton 的 Drum Rack 加上一個 MIDI clip，或 Logic 的 Step Sequencer。照著上面的其中一個節奏輸入。
  2. Find the swing (or groove) control and try 0%, 25% and 50%. 找到 swing（或 groove）控制，試試 0%、25%、50%。
  3. Make your own kick: any synth, sine wave, short amp decay, plus a pitch envelope that falls fast. In samplers, put open and closed hats in the same choke group. 自己做一個 kick：任何合成器、正弦波、短的音量衰減，再加上一個快速下降的音高包絡。在取樣器裡，把開放和閉合 hi-hat 放進同一個 choke group。

Key takeaways重點整理

  • Kick = falling sine. Snare = tone + noise. Hi-hat = high-passed noise.Kick＝下降的正弦波。Snare＝音＋噪音。Hi-hat＝高通過的噪音。
  • A bar of 4/4 = 16 steps. BPM sets how long each step lasts.一小節 4/4 拍＝16 步。BPM 決定每一步多長。
  • Backbeat (snare on 2 and 4) is the backbone of pop; swing adds groove.反拍（snare 在第 2、4 拍）是流行音樂的骨幹；swing 帶來律動。
