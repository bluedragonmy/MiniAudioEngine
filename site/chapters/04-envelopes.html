The shape of a sound聲音的形狀

Same oscillator, same filter — and yet one patch plucks like a harp while another swells like strings. The difference is how the volume moves over time. 同一個振盪器、同一個濾波器，一個音色像豎琴一樣撥出來，另一個卻像弦樂一樣慢慢湧上來。差別在於音量隨時間怎麼變化。

Four numbers: A, D, S, R四個數字：A、D、S、R

Almost every synth and sampler shapes its volume with an envelope that has four stages: 幾乎所有合成器和取樣器，都用一個有四個階段的包絡（envelope）來塑造音量：

  • Attack — how long it takes to reach full volume after you press the key.Attack（起音）：按下琴鍵後，到達最大音量要多久。
  • Decay — how long it takes to fall from full volume to the sustain level.Decay（衰減）：從最大音量降到持續音量要多久。
  • Sustain — the volume it holds while you keep the key down. This one is a level, not a time.Sustain（持續）：按住琴鍵時保持的音量。注意這個是音量，不是時間。
  • Release — how long it takes to fade out after you let go.Release（釋放）：放開琴鍵後，淡出到無聲要多久。
Try it: shape a note動手試：塑造一個音
Stage階段 — Level音量 0.00

Press and hold the button (or hold the space bar while it's focused). Let go to hear the release. 按住按鈕不放（或在按鈕被選取時按住空白鍵），放開就會聽到 release。

Try the presets and look at their shapes. A pluck has no sustain at all — the sound dies away even if you keep holding. A pad fades in slowly and lingers after you let go. An organ is a plain on/off switch. 試試看預設，比較它們的形狀。撥弦完全沒有 sustain，就算一直按著，聲音也會自己消失。長音墊慢慢淡入，放開後還會留一陣子。風琴就是單純的開和關。

Why "Attack = 0" can click為什麼「Attack = 0」會有爆音

Set attack and release to zero and play the organ a few times. Hear the small click at the start and end? The volume jumps instantly, and a sudden jump in a waveform contains energy at every frequency. That's why even "instant" envelopes in good synths take a few milliseconds. 把 attack 和 release 都調到 0，用風琴音色彈幾次。有沒有聽到開頭和結尾的小小「喀」聲？音量瞬間跳變，而波形上突然的跳變包含了所有頻率的能量。所以好的合成器裡，就算是「瞬間」的包絡，也會花上幾毫秒。

Train your ears練練耳朵

You'll hear one note held for about a second, then released. Which preset made it? 你會聽到一個音按住大約一秒後放開。它是哪一個預設？

Which envelope?哪一個包絡？
Envelopes are everywhere包絡無所不在

Here the envelope controls volume, but synths let you point an envelope at almost anything. An envelope on the filter cutoff (chapter 3) makes the sound bright at the start and darker as it decays — that's how most synth basses and plucks get their "bite". In mixing, a compressor's attack and release knobs use the very same idea. 這裡的包絡控制的是音量，但合成器幾乎可以把包絡接到任何參數上。把包絡接到濾波器截止頻率（第 3 章），聲音會在一開始很亮、隨著衰減變暗，大部分合成器貝斯和撥弦音的「咬勁」就是這樣來的。混音時，壓縮器的 attack 和 release 旋鈕也是同樣的概念。

Now try it in your DAW換你在 DAW 裡試試
  1. Open a synth and find the Amp (or Volume) envelope. In Ableton Analog it's under Amp; in FL Studio 3x Osc, open the channel's Envelope/Instrument tab and enable the volume envelope; in Logic Retro Synth it's the Amp envelope at the bottom. 開一個合成器，找到 Amp（或 Volume）包絡。Ableton Analog 在 Amp 區；FL Studio 3x Osc 要打開通道的 Envelope/Instrument 分頁，啟用音量包絡；Logic Retro Synth 在底部的 Amp 包絡。
  2. Recreate the four presets from this page with the same saw oscillator. Play a chord with each one. 用同一個鋸齒波振盪器，做出這一頁的四個預設，每一個都彈一個和弦聽聽看。
  3. Load a drum sample into a sampler (Ableton: Simpler, FL Studio: Sampler, Logic: Quick Sampler) and shorten its decay to make the hit tighter. 把一個鼓取樣載入取樣器（Ableton：Simpler，FL Studio：Sampler，Logic：Quick Sampler），把 decay 縮短，讓打擊聲更緊實。

Key takeaways重點整理

  • ADSR: three times (attack, decay, release) and one level (sustain).ADSR：三個時間（attack、decay、release）和一個音量（sustain）。
  • Short attack + no sustain = pluck. Long attack + long release = pad.短 attack＋沒有 sustain＝撥弦。長 attack＋長 release＝長音墊。
  • Instant volume changes click; give them a few milliseconds.音量瞬間變化會有爆音，給它幾毫秒的時間。
