Timbre & harmonics音色與泛音

A piano and a guitar can play the same note and still sound nothing alike. The difference is hiding above the note, in its harmonics. 鋼琴和吉他可以彈同一個音，聽起來卻完全不同。差別藏在那個音的上方，也就是它的泛音裡。

Every sound is a stack of sines每個聲音都是一疊正弦波

In chapter 1 you heard a pure sine. Real instruments are never that simple. When you play A2 (110 Hz), the string also vibrates at 220, 330, 440 Hz and so on — whole-number multiples of the note called harmonics. The note you name is the lowest one, the fundamental. How loud each harmonic is gives the sound its character: its timbre. 第 1 章聽到的是純正弦波，但真實的樂器從來沒有這麼單純。彈 A2（110 Hz）的時候，弦同時也在 220、330、440 Hz……振動，這些整數倍的頻率叫做泛音。我們叫得出名字的那個音是最低的一個，叫基音。每個泛音有多大聲，決定了聲音的個性，也就是音色。

Synths start from a few classic shapes. Each one is just a recipe for how loud the harmonics are. Pick a shape, then add harmonics one at a time and watch the wave take shape. 合成器從幾種經典波形出發，每一種其實就是一份「各個泛音要多大聲」的食譜。選一個波形，再一個一個加入泛音，看波形如何慢慢成形。

Try it: build a wave from harmonics動手試：用泛音疊出波形

  • Saw — every harmonic, each a bit quieter than the last. Bright and buzzy: the starting point for strings, brass and most synth leads.鋸齒波：所有泛音都有，一個比一個小聲一點。明亮、有嗡嗡聲，是弦樂、銅管和大部分合成器主旋律的起點。
  • Square — only the odd harmonics (1, 3, 5…). Hollow and woody, a bit like a clarinet.方波：只有奇數泛音（1、3、5……）。聽起來空空的、有木頭感，有點像單簧管。
  • Triangle — odd harmonics too, but they fade out fast. Soft, close to a flute or an ocarina.三角波：也只有奇數泛音，但衰減得很快。柔和，接近長笛或陶笛。

Notice the little ripples next to the sharp corners of the square and saw? Adding more harmonics squeezes them closer to the edge but never removes them. That's called the Gibbs phenomenon. We also stop adding harmonics at the Nyquist limit from chapter 2, so this synth can't alias. 注意到方波和鋸齒波尖角旁邊的小波紋了嗎？泛音加得越多，波紋越擠向邊緣，但永遠不會消失，這叫做 Gibbs 現象。另外，泛音只疊到第 2 章講的 Nyquist 上限為止，所以這個合成器不會產生 aliasing。

Filters remove harmonics濾波器拿掉泛音

If timbre is a recipe of harmonics, the quickest way to change it is to take some away. That's what a filter does. A low-pass filter lets everything below its cutoff through and turns down what's above, so the sound gets darker and rounder. Start with a bright saw and carve it down — this is called subtractive synthesis, and it's how most synths work. 如果音色是一份泛音食譜，最快改變它的方法就是拿掉一些泛音，這就是濾波器的工作。低通濾波器讓截止頻率（cutoff）以下的聲音通過，把以上的部分調小，聲音就會變暗、變圓潤。從明亮的鋸齒波開始往下削，這叫做減法合成，大部分合成器都是這樣運作的。

Try it: filter a saw wave動手試：替鋸齒波濾波

Play, then drag the cutoff slowly from top to bottom. With the steep filter, turn the resonance up and try again: the peak at the cutoff is the classic "wow" of a synth filter sweep. Resonance only applies to the steep filter. 按下播放，再把截止頻率從上往下慢慢拉。換成陡峭的濾波器，把共振調高再試一次：截止頻率附近凸起的那一塊，就是合成器濾波掃頻那種經典的「哇」聲。共振只對陡峭的濾波器有作用。

The two filters differ in slope: how fast they turn things down past the cutoff. The gentle one loses 6 dB for every octave; the steep one loses 12. Steeper slopes sound more obviously "filtered". Synths often go to 24 dB/oct by chaining two steep filters. 兩種濾波器的差別在於斜率：超過截止頻率後衰減得多快。平緩的每高一個八度少 6 dB，陡峭的少 12 dB。斜率越陡，「被濾過」的感覺越明顯。合成器常把兩個陡峭濾波器串起來，做到 24 dB/oct。

Same idea, different job: EQ同樣的原理，不同的用途：EQ

The EQ on every mixer channel is a set of these filters. A high-pass (the mirror image: removes lows) on a vocal or guitar clears out rumble that only muddies the mix. A gentle low-pass on a harsh cymbal softens it. You'll use this constantly in chapter 6. 每個混音軌道上的 EQ，就是一組這樣的濾波器。在人聲或吉他上加一個高通濾波器（低通的相反：拿掉低頻），可以清掉只會讓混音變糊的低頻隆隆聲。在刺耳的鈸上加一點平緩的低通，可以讓它柔和一點。第 6 章會常常用到。

Now try it in your DAW換你在 DAW 裡試試
  1. Open a basic synth (Ableton: Analog or Wavetable, FL Studio: 3x Osc, Logic: Retro Synth) and switch between sine, saw and square while holding a note. Watch a spectrum analyzer on the same track. 開一個基本合成器（Ableton：Analog 或 Wavetable，FL Studio：3x Osc，Logic：Retro Synth），按住一個音，在正弦波、鋸齒波、方波之間切換，同時看同一軌上的頻譜分析器。
  2. Find the synth's filter. Set it to low-pass, then sweep Cutoff and Resonance (sometimes called Res or Q). 找到合成器的濾波器，設成低通（low-pass），然後轉動 Cutoff 和 Resonance（有時叫 Res 或 Q）。
  3. Automate the cutoff so it opens slowly over 8 bars. You've just made a filter sweep, one of the most common moves in electronic music. 替 cutoff 畫自動化，讓它在 8 個小節內慢慢打開。你剛做出了濾波掃頻，這是電子音樂裡最常見的手法之一。

Key takeaways重點整理

  • A note = a fundamental + harmonics at whole-number multiples.一個音＝基音＋整數倍頻率的泛音。
  • Timbre is the balance of harmonics. Saw = all, square = odd, triangle = odd and quiet.音色就是各個泛音的比例。鋸齒波＝全部都有，方波＝只有奇數，三角波＝奇數而且很小聲。
  • A low-pass filter removes harmonics above its cutoff; resonance boosts the cutoff itself.低通濾波器拿掉截止頻率以上的泛音；共振會把截止頻率附近推高。
