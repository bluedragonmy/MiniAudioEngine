Mixing basics混音入門

Mixing is making every part of a track audible at once. Most beginner mixes don't fail because of a missing plugin — they fail on levels, on two sounds fighting for the same space, or on phase. 混音就是讓每個聲部能同時被聽清楚。大部分新手的混音出問題，不是因為少了哪個外掛，而是音量、兩個聲音搶同一塊空間，或是相位。

Levels, panning and the 0 dB ceiling音量、聲像與 0 dB 天花板

Every track goes through a fader (its level, in dB) and a pan knob (where it sits between left and right), then everything adds up on the master. Remember chapter 1: the master can't go above 0 dBFS. When tracks pile up past that, the peaks get chopped off — clipping. 每一軌都會經過推桿（音量，單位 dB）和聲像（pan）旋鈕（放在左右之間的哪裡），最後全部加總到 master。還記得第 1 章嗎？master 不能超過 0 dBFS。各軌疊起來超過的時候，波峰就會被削掉，這就是削波（clipping）。

Try it: a four-track mixer動手試：四軌混音台
Master—

Push every fader up to +6 dB and listen to the master: the crunch is clipping. Then pull everything back down — a mix sounds just as good quieter, you simply turn up your speakers. 把每個推桿都推到 +6 dB，聽聽 master：那種破掉的沙沙聲就是削波。再把全部拉回來，混音小聲一點聽起來一樣好，把喇叭開大聲就行了。

Gain staging, in one sentenceGain staging，一句話講完

Leave room: aim for master peaks around −6 dBFS while mixing, and if everything is too loud, pull the faders down instead of reaching for a limiter. Loudness comes later, in chapter 7. 留點空間：混音時讓 master 峰值大約在 −6 dBFS，如果全部都太大聲，就把推桿拉下來，而不是直接加一個 limiter。響度是第 7 章的事。

Why your kick and bass are fighting為什麼你的 kick 和 bass 在打架

This is the most common beginner complaint: the low end is "muddy" and the kick disappears. The reason is masking. The kick's boom and the bass note both live around 40–100 Hz. When two sounds share a frequency range, the louder one hides the quieter one, and together they blur. 這是新手最常見的抱怨：低頻「糊糊的」，kick 不見了。原因是遮蔽（masking）。kick 的低音和 bass 的音符都在 40–100 Hz 附近。兩個聲音佔用同一個頻段時，大聲的會蓋住小聲的，混在一起就糊掉了。

There are two classic fixes. EQ carving turns the bass down a little right where the kick lives. Sidechain ducking turns the bass down for a split second every time the kick hits, using the kick's own envelope. Try each on its own, then both. 有兩種經典解法。EQ 挖空：在 kick 所在的頻率，把 bass 調小一點。Sidechain 閃避（ducking）：每次 kick 打下去的瞬間，用 kick 自己的包絡把 bass 壓低一下。分別試試看，再兩個一起開。

Phase: when two kicks make less than one相位：兩個 kick 加起來比一個還小

Layering two kicks to make a bigger one sounds like a good idea. But sound is pressure going up and down: if one kick is pushing while the other is pulling, they cancel. A tiny delay between them (a few milliseconds), or one being upside down (polarity flipped), can make the layer thinner than either kick alone. 把兩個 kick 疊起來做出更大的 kick，聽起來是好主意。但聲音是壓力的上上下下：如果一個在推、另一個在拉，它們就會互相抵消。兩者之間差了一點點時間（幾毫秒），或其中一個上下顛倒（極性反轉），疊出來的聲音反而會比單一個 kick 更薄。

Flip the polarity with no delay and the two kicks cancel completely: silence. Then try delays around 5–10 ms. 不延遲、直接反轉極性，兩個 kick 會完全抵消：一片安靜。然後試試 5–10 毫秒左右的延遲。

Now try it in your DAW換你在 DAW 裡試試
  1. Gain staging: play your loudest section and watch the master meter. If it hits red, select all tracks and pull the faders down together. Gain staging：播放最大聲的段落，看 master 音量表。如果亮紅燈，全選所有軌道，一起把推桿拉下來。
  2. Sidechain: put a compressor on the bass and feed it the kick (Ableton: Compressor → Sidechain; Logic: Compressor → Side Chain menu; FL Studio: Fruity Limiter in COMP mode with the kick's mixer track routed to it as sidechain). Sidechain：在 bass 上加壓縮器，讓 kick 觸發它（Ableton：Compressor → Sidechain；Logic：Compressor → Side Chain 選單；FL Studio：Fruity Limiter 切到 COMP 模式，把 kick 的混音軌以 sidechain 方式送過去）。
  3. Phase: when you layer two kicks or record one source with two mics, flip the polarity of one (Ableton: Utility → Phz; Logic: Gain plugin → Phase Invert; FL Studio: the mixer track's polarity button) and keep whichever sounds fuller. 相位：疊兩個 kick，或用兩支麥克風錄同一個聲源時，把其中一個的極性反轉（Ableton：Utility → Phz；Logic：Gain 外掛 → Phase Invert；FL Studio：混音軌上的極性按鈕），留下聽起來比較飽滿的那個。

Key takeaways重點整理

  • Tracks add up on the master; past 0 dBFS they clip. Mix with headroom.各軌在 master 加總，超過 0 dBFS 就會削波。混音時要留餘裕（headroom）。
  • Muddy low end is usually masking: carve with EQ or duck with sidechain.低頻糊掉通常是遮蔽：用 EQ 挖空，或用 sidechain 閃避。
  • Layers can cancel. Check polarity and timing whenever you stack similar sounds.疊加可能互相抵消。疊類似的聲音時，記得檢查極性和時間差。

Under the hood: the loop is rendered with DrumSynth, AdditiveOscillator, Biquad (the 55 Hz cut is a peaking EQ) and ADSR; panning uses the constant-power law from DrumVoice.cpp. The sidechain is a simple envelope follower on the kick. 技術細節：loop 由 DrumSynth、AdditiveOscillator、Biquad（55 Hz 的挖空是一個 peaking EQ）和 ADSR 算出來；聲像使用 DrumVoice.cpp 裡的等功率定律。sidechain 是對 kick 做的簡單包絡追蹤。
